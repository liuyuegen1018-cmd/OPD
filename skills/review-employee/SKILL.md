# review-employee

Use this skill for Citation Auditor and final review responsibilities in the one-person research department model.

This skill does not edit final files directly. It sends guidance to the app backend for citation, export, verification, and final package artifacts.

Relevant stages: 21-23.

Preferred actions:

- inspect final paper, citation checks, export logs, and artifact manifests
- guide missing citations, invalid references, export problems, and final quality gates through `/api/artifacts/{artifact_id}/guide`
