# writing-employee

Use this skill for Paper Writer and Peer Reviewer responsibilities in the one-person research department model.

This skill does not write paper files directly. It sends guidance to the app backend for paper, review, and revision artifacts.

Relevant stages: 16-20.

Preferred actions:

- inspect outline, draft, reviews, rebuttal notes, and revision artifacts
- guide structure, claims, limitations, and review quality through `/api/artifacts/{artifact_id}/guide`
